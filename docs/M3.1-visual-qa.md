# M3.1 visual QA

## Findings

The hierarchy and semantic bindings were present. The resolver converted local `x` joint offsets to pixels using `min(width, height)`, but left local `y` offsets in rig units. Vertical joint spacing therefore collapsed to a few tenths of a pixel. Artwork parts were bound and drawn in their declared `z_index` order, but torso and leg shapes overlapped near the pelvis and were effectively hidden; this was a rig coordinate conversion defect, not a missing asset or canvas clip.

The live preview also places a lower-third over the bottom of the scene. The fixture had the character off-center in landscape and placed the feet in that lower-third area. Both aspect variants now position the character centrally, preserve the full-body bounds above the lower-third, and place the floor at the visible foot line.

## Change

`resolveHumanRig` now converts both local axes to isotropic pixels before applying parent rotation and scale. The appearance renderer retains per-part semantic bindings and painter order. Its optional `{ debugHumanRig: true }` render option overlays every resolved joint and parent link after artwork; the overlay does not change the resolved pose.

The M3.1 idle fixture retains its legacy rig parts, with independent landscape and portrait character transforms and floor placement. The head, hands, and feet are checked against safe canvas bounds for all three appearances in each ratio.

## Verification

`npm test` passes, including rig coordinate, overlay, appearance binding, and layout-bound regressions. `npm run test:focused` passes (8/8), and `git diff --check` is clean. M3.1 visual QA is complete; M3.2 remains unstarted.
